# Mysterious Egg

Parent: [Items and Crafting](/mod/hydragon/items-and-crafting-index) | [Home](/mod/hydragon/)

The Mysterious Egg is the crafted start of your personal Soul Bond. Make it at the **Draconic Altar** in the **Bonding** category, then hatch it when you are ready to claim the one lifelong Miniwyvern bond available to your player.

## Recipe

The recipe creates one Mysterious Egg:

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Plain Draconic Essence | 3 |
| Earth Draconic Essence | 3 |
| Fire Draconic Essence | 3 |
| Ice Draconic Essence | 3 |
| Lightning Draconic Essence | 3 |
| Nature Draconic Essence | 3 |
| Toxic Draconic Essence | 3 |
| Void Draconic Essence | 3 |
| Wind Draconic Essence | 3 |
| Draconic Scale | 10 |

Earth and Wind essences are required crafting inputs here; neither is a Miniwyvern form.

## Hatch once, bond for life

Hatching the egg claims your single lifelong Soul Bond. The new Miniwyvern starts in the **Wild** form and joins the Dragon Command Flute roster. A later Mysterious Egg cannot create a second personal Miniwyvern after your bond is claimed.

The [Soul Bond and Attunement](/mod/hydragon/soul-bond-and-attunement) guide explains re-attunement, while the [Soulbound Miniwyvern](/mod/hydragon/soulbound-miniwyvern) page covers growth and combat.

## Do not confuse the two eggs

The **Drake Egg** is a separate decorative block that may appear as a rare optional dragon drop. It can be placed as scenery but cannot be hatched, cannot claim a Soul Bond, and cannot replace the crafted Mysterious Egg.
