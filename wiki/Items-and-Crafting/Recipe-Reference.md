## Bonding (Soul Bond)

| Recipe | Station | Ingredients | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Mysterious Egg](/mod/hydragon/mysterious-egg) | Draconic Altar — Bonding | 3 each plain, Earth, Fire, Ice, Lightning, Nature, Toxic, Void, and Wind Draconic Essences + 10 Draconic Scales | 1 Mysterious Egg |

Hatching the Mysterious Egg claims the one-per-player lifelong Soul Bond; the decorative Drake Egg is a separate, non-hatching item.

## Attunement Materials

Each conversion below creates one elemental Draconic Essence and consumes 2 plain Draconic Essences plus the listed ingredient. Earth and Wind are crafting essences, not Miniwyvern forms.
