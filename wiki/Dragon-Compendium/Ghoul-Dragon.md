# Ghoul Dragon

Parent: [Dragon Compendium](/mod/hydragon/dragon-compendium-index) | [Home](/mod/hydragon/)

Ghoul Dragons are flying melee predators found in **Zone 2 during the day and night**. Search soil and sand in savannas, plateaus, scrublands, deserts, and oases.

They alternate ground roaming and flight. They land to fight grounded targets, but stay airborne or take off to pursue airborne targets. Both modes use left and right claw swipes and a bite, with no breath attacks or projectiles. A short delay prevents brief jumps or touchdown flicker from repeatedly switching combat modes.

## Capture and companionship

Tranquilize and capture a Ghoul Dragon with a [Draconic Stone](/mod/hydragon/draconic-stones), then summon it through the [Dragon Command Flute](/mod/hydragon/dragon-command-flute). It supports the standard follow, hold, defend, aggressive, attack-target, move, recall, and home commands.

Ghoul Dragons keep their skin after capture. The four appearances are Brilliant Blue, Savanna, Savanna Dusk, and Silver Moon.

## Flight and progression

Use the **Flightmaster's Talisman** to mount and fly. Launch, boost, glide, airbrake, and Vigour work like the other major flying companions. Mounted flight has no ranged combat abilities.

Combat and mounted flight award experience, up to level 30. Talents support aerial travel, melee damage, health and protection, and longer summons with shorter cooldowns.

A defeated bonded Ghoul Dragon can be revived for **2 Revitalizing Essences and 2 Void Draconic Essences** through the normal companion revival flow.
