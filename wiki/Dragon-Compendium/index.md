## Suggested Reading Path

1. Compare the [Rock Drakes](/mod/hydragon/rock-drakes) if you are starting with a ground mount.
2. Read the [Hydra](/mod/hydragon/hydra), [Toxic Hydra](/mod/hydragon/toxic-hydra), and [Nordic Drake](/mod/hydragon/nordic-drake) pages before planning a harder expedition.
3. Follow the [Soulbound Miniwyvern](/mod/hydragon/soulbound-miniwyvern) route when you are ready to craft the Mysterious Egg.
4. Choose a path on [Miniwyvern Forms](/mod/hydragon/miniwyvern-forms) after the Soul Bond is active.

For a condensed location, movement, and drop lookup, use [Quick Reference](/mod/hydragon/quick-reference). If a capture or spawn result is confusing, see [FAQ and Troubleshooting](/mod/hydragon/faq-and-troubleshooting).
