# Nordic Drake

Parent: [Dragon Compendium](/mod/hydragon/dragon-compendium-index) | [Home](/mod/hydragon/)

The Nordic Drake is a high-difficulty fire dragon and the roster's full avatar-flight mount. It rewards careful capture preparation and talent choices that support either aerial travel or direct combat.

## Where to find it

Search **Zone 3 forests on non-snowy soil**. If the ground is covered in snow, keep looking for a forest patch with ordinary soil.

## How to obtain it

1. Bring a tranquilizer and a **Draconic Stone** suited to a difficult full-dragon hunt.
2. Work the Nordic Drake down safely, then tranquilize it before you begin the capture channel.
3. Hold the stone's action through the channel. A failed attempt does not make the dragon tame, so plan your supplies and a safe retreat before you try again.
4. On success, summon the bonded Nordic Drake from the [Dragon Command Flute](/mod/hydragon/dragon-command-flute).

The Nordic Drake has a high capture difficulty. Stronger stones are a sensible recommendation, not a published hard minimum; the full [Capturing Dragons guide](/mod/hydragon/capturing-dragons) covers the shared process.

## Wild attacks

Expect two fire signatures while you fight it:

- **Fireballs:** ranged projectiles that pressure you while you reposition.
- **Flame breath:** a close, sweeping attack during a pass.

Do not stand in front of the Drake while it lines up a breath attack. Use cover and keep room to dodge the fireball launches.

## Bonded combat and avatar flight

The bonded Nordic Drake keeps its fireball and flame-breath attacks. It also grants **avatar flight**, letting you ride as the player instead of steering a separate flying creature. Flight uses a **Vigour** resource: boost and climb while it is available, then glide and let it recharge before the next long push.

## Leveling and talent identity

Spend Nordic Drake talent points around one of four clear identities:

| Branch | Best fit |
|---|---|
| Aerial Mastery | More flight Vigour, better recharge, boosts, gliding, and climbing. |
| War Drake | Stronger outgoing combat pressure with added resilience. |
| Wyrmguard | Health and damage mitigation for safer hunts. |
| Summoner's Pact | Longer summon windows and shorter return cooldowns. |

The [Leveling and Talents guide](/mod/hydragon/leveling-and-talents) explains how to sequence branch choices as the Drake grows.

## Drops and progression value

The Nordic Drake's drops are useful for both capture progression and essence work. Treat each quantity as a range from a successful drop, not as a promise of a specific roll:

| Material | Drop range |
|---|---:|
| Draconic Scales | 6–8 |
| plain Draconic Essence | 6–8 |
| Ice Draconic Essence | 4–5 |
| Wind Draconic Essence | 2–3 |

A **Void Draconic Essence** is a rare additional drop and is not guaranteed.
