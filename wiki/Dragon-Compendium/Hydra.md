# Hydra

Parent: [Dragon Compendium](/mod/hydragon/dragon-compendium-index) | [Home](/mod/hydragon/)

The Hydra is a cold-weather full dragon with ranged ice attacks and a reliable ground-mount role. It is a good choice when you want a durable companion that can keep pressure on targets without giving up close-range attacks.

## Where to find it

Search **Zone 3 glacial ice-and-snow terrain**. The Hydra is seen more often during its favored moon phase, so a quiet search at the right time can be worth the trip.

## How to obtain it

1. Bring a tranquilizer, a **Draconic Stone**, and enough supplies for more than one attempt.
2. Find a wild Hydra in its glacial habitat and lower its health while you fight safely.
3. Tranquilize it, then channel the Draconic Stone while the capture window is open. The [Capturing Dragons guide](/mod/hydragon/capturing-dragons) explains the timing and what to do if the channel is interrupted.
4. On success, the bonded Hydra is stored in your [Dragon Command Flute](/mod/hydragon/dragon-command-flute) roster.

Every eligible stone can attempt a capture. Stronger stones are a sensible preparation as you move from early hunts to tougher full dragons, but there is no player-facing minimum tier to memorize.

## Wild attacks

Expect a mix of close-range strikes and two signature ice attacks:

- **Ice ball:** a focused projectile that gives the Hydra a way to punish targets at range.
- **Ice rain:** a spread of falling ice that pressures an area instead of one target.

Keep moving when the Hydra begins an ice cast, and avoid standing in the rain's impact area.

## Bonded combat and mount

The bonded Hydra keeps its ice ball and ice-rain identity. It is a **ground mount**, so use it for travel and fighting on the terrain rather than for airborne movement. The flute's companion commands let you summon, dismiss, recall, and direct it; see [Mounts and Flight](/mod/hydragon/mounts-and-flight) for riding tips.

## Leveling and talent identity

The Hydra gains experience while it adventures with you. Spend its talent points to match the job you want it to do:

| Branch | Best fit |
|---|---|
| Winter's Wrath | More ice pressure and damage in combat. |
| Glacierguard | More health and protection from incoming damage. |
| Broodcaller's Pact | Longer summon windows and faster returns after cooldowns. |

Read [Leveling and Talents](/mod/hydragon/leveling-and-talents) before spending points if you want to commit to a single role.

## Drops and progression value

Defeating a wild Hydra can supply the materials used to improve stones, attunements, and companion care:

| Material | Typical drop range |
|---|---:|
| Draconic Scales | 5–6 |
| plain Draconic Essence | 5–6 |
| Ice Draconic Essence | 3–4 |
| Earth Draconic Essence | 2–3 |

Void Draconic Essence and the decorative **Drake Egg** are possible additional drops. The Drake Egg is not the crafted [Mysterious Egg](/mod/hydragon/mysterious-egg), so keep those two items separate when planning your progression.
