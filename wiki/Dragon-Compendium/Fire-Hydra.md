# Fire Hydra

Parent: [Dragon Compendium](/mod/hydragon/dragon-compendium-index) | [Home](/mod/hydragon/)

The Fire Hydra is a ground-dwelling variant of the [Ice Hydra](/mod/hydragon/hydra), with dark scales, glowing red markings, fireballs, and burning rain.

## Where to find it

Search volcanic ground in **Zone 4's overworld volcanoes**. Like the Ice Hydra, it appears more often during its favored moon phase.

## Capture and combat

Lower its health, tranquilize it, and channel a [Draconic Stone](/mod/hydragon/draconic-stones) to capture it. A successful capture adds it to your [Dragon Command Flute](/mod/hydragon/dragon-command-flute) roster.

The wild Fire Hydra keeps the Ice Hydra's health, movement, melee attacks, and casting rhythm. Its ranged burst launches three fireballs that burn nearby targets. Its rain attack scatters explosive fireballs and leaves burning hazards around their impact points. Keep moving during casts and leave the impact areas.

The bonded Fire Hydra uses the same ground companion behavior and reduced attack damage as the bonded Ice Hydra. It supports ground riding, commands, breeding, leveling, and talents.

## Talents and revival

Choose **Firestorm** for damage, **Ashen Guard** for health and protection, or **Broodcaller's Pact** for summon improvements. See [Leveling and Talents](/mod/hydragon/leveling-and-talents).

Revival costs **2 Revitalizing Essences and 2 Fire Draconic Essences**. See [Summoning and Revival](/mod/hydragon/summoning-and-revival).

## Drops

- 5–6 Draconic Scales.
- 5–6 plain Draconic Essences.
- 3–4 Fire Draconic Essences.
- 2–3 Earth Draconic Essences.
- A chance of 1 Void Draconic Essence.
