# Quick Reference

Parent: [Help and Reference](/mod/hydragon/help-and-reference-index) | [Home](/mod/hydragon/)

Use these compact tables to choose a hunt, prepare a stone, check a companion's movement, or plan recovery. Each row links to the page with the full explanation.

## Locations and suggested stones

The stone column is a practical progression, not a hard minimum. Every eligible full dragon can be attempted with every stone tier; stronger stones improve the chance of a resolved success.

## Primary drops

These are the common family materials and guaranteed elemental identities. Void essences and decorative Drake Eggs are occasional bonuses where listed; the Toxic Hydra never drops a Drake Egg.

| Creature | Primary drops | Details |
|---|---|---|

| [Toxic Hydra](/mod/hydragon/toxic-hydra) | 5–6 scales, 5–6 plain essence, 3–4 Toxic essence, 2–3 Earth essence; possible Void; **no Drake Egg** | [Toxic Hydra](/mod/hydragon/toxic-hydra) |
| [Nordic Drake](/mod/hydragon/nordic-drake) | 6–8 scales, 6–8 plain essence, 4–5 Ice essence, 2–3 Wind essence; rare Void | [Nordic Drake](/mod/hydragon/nordic-drake) |

## Active limits

| Companion family | Owned limit | Active limit | Details |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| [Full dragons](/mod/hydragon/summoning-and-revival) | Unlimited | 1 | [Summoning and Revival](/mod/hydragon/summoning-and-revival) |
| [Soulbound Miniwyvern](/mod/hydragon/summoning-and-revival) | 1 per player | 1 | [Summoning and Revival](/mod/hydragon/summoning-and-revival) |

The two active slots are independent, so one full dragon and one Miniwyvern can be active together.
