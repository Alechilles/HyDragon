# Capturing Dragons

Parent: [Companion Guides](/mod/hydragon/companion-guides-index) | [Home](/mod/hydragon/)

Capture applies to eligible full dragons: Hydra, Toxic Hydra, Nordic Drake, and the Young, standard, and Frost Rock Drakes. A Miniwyvern is created through a Soul Bond instead; see [Soul Bond and Attunement](/mod/hydragon/soul-bond-and-attunement).

## Before you start

- Carry the **Dragon Command Flute** so the new companion can be stored in your roster.
- Make sure the flute can accept another stored companion and keep a safe retreat route.
- Find an eligible full dragon and lower its health. Lower health improves the chance of a successful attempt.
- Tranquilize the dragon before channeling.
- Carry a **Draconic Stone** and stay close enough for the channel to remain connected.

## Capture steps

1. Fight the dragon until its health is low enough for a sensible attempt.
2. Tranquilize it and wait for the capture opening.
3. Hold the Draconic Stone's capture action for the full **three-second channel**. Releasing early or losing the target interrupts the attempt.
4. Keep the dragon tranquilized, remain in range, and keep the flute and roster requirements valid until the channel resolves.
5. A success bonds the dragon to you and places it in the Dragon Command Flute roster.

### When is a stone consumed?

| Outcome | Stone result |
|---|---|
| You release the action, lose range, or the prerequisites become invalid | No stone is consumed. |
| The channel resolves as a success | One stone is consumed. |
| The channel resolves as a failed capture | One stone is consumed. |

An interrupted or invalid attempt is different from a resolved failure: only a resolved success or failure spends the stone.

## Suggested stone progression

Every stone tier can attempt every eligible full dragon. Stronger stones improve the chance, but the list below is a practical progression rather than a set of hard gates.

| Stone | A sensible target |
|---|---|
| **Iron** | Young Rock Drake |
| **Thorium** | Rock Drake |
| **Cobalt** | Frost Rock Drake or Hydra |
| **Adamantium** | Nordic Drake and other difficult hunts |
| **Ancient** | A guaranteed valid attempt after all prerequisites remain satisfied |

You do not need a mandatory minimum stone tier for a species. An Ancient stone still requires the Dragon Command Flute, an eligible full dragon, tranquilization, a valid roster, and a connected channel.

## Improve your odds

Lower the dragon's health without killing it, keep it tranquilized, and use a stronger stone when you can afford one. Plan extra stones for resolved failures; you keep the stone when an attempt is interrupted or invalid because it never reaches a resolution.
