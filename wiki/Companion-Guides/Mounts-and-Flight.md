# Mounts and Flight

Parent: [Companion Guides](/mod/hydragon/companion-guides-index) | [Home](/mod/hydragon/)

HyDragon has two kinds of travel companion: ground-mounted full dragons and full dragons that let you fly as the rider avatar.

## Ground mounts

The standard Hydra and every Rock Drake are **ground mounts**. They carry you across terrain but do not switch into an airborne avatar. Rock Drakes share their boulder attack identity; the Hydra keeps its ice attacks. Use [Dragon Command Flute](/mod/hydragon/dragon-command-flute) commands to recall a ground mount before a steep climb or a tight cave.

## Avatar-flight mounts

| Companion | Flight experience |
|---|---|
| **Nordic Drake** | Full avatar flight with fire abilities and a dedicated flight talent branch. |
| **Winged Toxic Hydra** | A separate airborne Toxic Hydra mode with toxic ranged and breath abilities. |

Avatar flight changes your riding view and movement; you are flying as the companion rather than directing a separate wild creature. Summon your dragon, select the **Flightmaster's Talisman**, then **crouch and interact** with the dragon to mount.

## Flight controls

Nordic Drake and Winged Toxic Hydra share these avatar-flight controls. The mouse buttons and keys below assume default bindings.

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| **Charge and launch** | While mounted on the ground, hold crouch to charge, then release to launch. |
| **Flap upward** | Left-click (primary action). |
| **Airbrake** | Right-click (secondary action). |
| **Forward boost** | Press **Q** (Ability 1). |
| **Dismount** | Press **F** (interact). This also dismounts immediately while airborne. |

Steer with your movement controls and look direction. Glide between flaps and boosts, and watch your **Vigour** so you have energy for the next climb or escape. Land before dismounting if you want to stay on the ground.

The standard Hydra and Rock Drakes remain ground mounts.

## Talent choices for travel

- **Nordic Drake — Aerial Mastery:** improve Vigour capacity and recharge, boost efficiency and strength, glide control, and climbing.
- **Ice Hydra:** **Winter's Wrath** is the combat tree, **Glacierguard** is the durability tree, and **Broodcaller's Pact** supplies the summon milestones.
- **Winged Toxic Hydra:** **Plaguewing** is the flight tree, **Venomous Onslaught** is the combat tree, **Blightguard** is the durability tree, and **Broodcaller's Pact** supplies the summon milestones.
- **Ground mounts:** invest in combat, durability, or survival branches instead of flight-specific choices.

See [Leveling and Talents](/mod/hydragon/leveling-and-talents) before committing points, and [Summoning and Revival](/mod/hydragon/summoning-and-revival) for the full-dragon summon window.
