# Mounts and Flight

Parent: [Companion Guides](/mod/hydragon/companion-guides-index) | [Home](/mod/hydragon/)

HyDragon has two kinds of travel companion: ground-mounted full dragons and full dragons that let you fly as the rider avatar.

## Ground mounts

The standard Hydra and every Rock Drake are **ground mounts**. They carry you across terrain but do not switch into an airborne avatar. Rock Drakes share their boulder attack identity; the Hydra keeps its ice attacks. Use [Dragon Command Flute](/mod/hydragon/dragon-command-flute) commands to recall a ground mount before a steep climb or a tight cave.

## Avatar-flight mounts

| Companion | Flight experience |
|---|---|
| **Nordic Drake** | Full avatar flight with fire abilities and a dedicated flight talent branch. |
| **Winged Toxic Hydra** | A separate airborne Toxic Hydra mode with toxic ranged and breath abilities. |

Avatar flight changes your riding view and movement; you are flying as the companion rather than directing a separate wild creature. Use the flute's airborne toggle when the companion is active and the control is available.

## Flight controls

The shared flight controls are easy to remember:

- Hold jump to flap or climb.
- Use sprint for a forward boost.
- Use crouch to air-brake.
- Glide between boosts and watch your **Vigour** so you have energy for the next climb or escape.

If a companion does not expose the airborne toggle, keep it in its ground role. The standard Hydra and Rock Drakes remain ground mounts.

## Talent choices for travel

- **Nordic Drake — Aerial Mastery:** improve Vigour capacity and recharge, boost efficiency and strength, glide control, and climbing.
- **Winged Toxic Hydra:** use its airborne toxic attacks for mounted combat, then choose from its available combat, durability, survival, and summon-focused talents.
- **Ground mounts:** invest in combat, durability, or survival branches instead of flight-specific choices.

See [Leveling and Talents](/mod/hydragon/leveling-and-talents) before committing points, and [Summoning and Revival](/mod/hydragon/summoning-and-revival) for the full-dragon summon window.
