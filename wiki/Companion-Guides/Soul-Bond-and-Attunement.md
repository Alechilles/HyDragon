# Soul Bond and Attunement

Parent: [Companion Guides](/mod/hydragon/companion-guides-index) | [Home](/mod/hydragon/)

Your Soul Bond creates one personal Miniwyvern. It is a separate path from capturing full dragons in the wild.

## Claim your one bond

1. Craft a **Mysterious Egg** at the Draconic Altar. See [Mysterious Egg](/mod/hydragon/mysterious-egg) for the recipe.
2. Hatch the egg to claim your lifelong bond. Each player can claim this bond once.
3. The new Miniwyvern starts in the **Wild** form and joins the Dragon Command Flute roster.

The bond is tied to your player, not to a temporary summon. A second egg cannot create a second personal Miniwyvern after the bond is claimed.

## Re-attune an owned Miniwyvern

Summon your owned Miniwyvern, hold the required essence, and interact with it. Each change uses eight essences:

| New form | Hold while interacting |
|---|---|
| **Fire** | 8 Fire Draconic Essences |
| **Ice** | 8 Ice Draconic Essences |
| **Lightning** | 8 Lightning Draconic Essences |
| **Nature** | 8 Nature Draconic Essences |
| **Toxic** | 8 Toxic Draconic Essences |
| **Void** | 8 Void Draconic Essences |
| **Wild** | 8 plain Draconic Essences |

The six elemental choices give the Miniwyvern a different form and combat identity. Eight plain Draconic Essences return it to Wild. Earth and Wind essences remain crafting materials, not selectable Miniwyvern forms.

## What persists through a form change?

Re-attunement changes the Miniwyvern's form and appearance without creating a replacement companion. Its level, ownership, and roster identity persist, so you can change roles without losing the companion you raised.

Compare the form identities on [Miniwyvern Forms](/mod/hydragon/miniwyvern-forms), then plan the talent branches in [Leveling and Talents](/mod/hydragon/leveling-and-talents).
