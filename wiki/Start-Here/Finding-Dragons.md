The Soulbound Miniwyvern is made through the [Soul Bond and Attunement guide](/mod/hydragon/soul-bond-and-attunement), not found as a wild spawn.

Wild Miniwyverns travel in hostile flocks of one to five, with one element per flock. All have 40 health and cannot be tamed. They do not replace the crafted-egg companion route.

| Wild variant | Where to search |
|---|---|
| Normal | Zone 1 plains |
| Fire | Zone 2 deserts; Zone 4 volcanoes and forests, on soil or volcanic rock |
| Ice | Zone 3 glaciers and snowy mountains; tundra soil |
| Nature | Zone 1 forests, autumn forests, and azure forests; Zone 2 oases; non-snowy Zone 3 forests; Zone 4 jungles |
| Toxic | Zone 1 swamp soil and mud; Zone 2 oases |
| Lightning | Zone 1 and Zone 3 mountains; Zone 2 plateaus |
| Void | Dark outdoor habitats across Zones 1–3 at night; they despawn during the day |

Elemental wild Miniwyverns drop matching elemental essence and use the same base projectile effects as their Soulbound counterparts. All wild variants share loose flight formations, thermal circling, short ground walks with rests, and airborne swoop attacks.
