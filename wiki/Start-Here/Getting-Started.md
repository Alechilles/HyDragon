# Getting Started

Parent: [Start Here Index](/mod/hydragon/start-here-index) | [Home](/mod/hydragon/)

Follow these steps in order to make your first dragon companion:

1. At a Workbench, craft a **Draconic Altar**. See the [Recipe Reference](/mod/hydragon/recipe-reference) for the recipe.
2. Use the altar to craft the **Dragon Command Flute**. The [Dragon Command Flute guide](/mod/hydragon/dragon-command-flute) explains its roster.
3. Prepare a tranquilizer and a **Draconic Stone** for the creature you want to capture.
4. Find an eligible full dragon using [Finding Dragons](/mod/hydragon/finding-dragons).
5. Tranquilize the dragon until it is ready to be captured.
6. Channel the Draconic Stone to complete the capture. See [Capturing Dragons](/mod/hydragon/capturing-dragons) for the full process.
7. Use the flute roster to summon and manage your new companion.
