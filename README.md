# HyDragon

### Raise dragons. Master the elements. Rule the skies.

HyDragon transforms your world into the ultimate dragon adventure. Seek out legendary beasts, earn their loyalty, raise powerful companions, and take flight alongside creatures worthy of myth.

Explore forests, volcanic wilds, moonlit lands, and deep caves in search of an incredible dragon roster:

- Command mighty Hydras and unleash storms of ice.
- Brave the venomous attacks of the airborne Toxic Hydra.
- Discover fire-breathing Nordic Drakes roaming the forests.
- Challenge increasingly powerful Rock Drakes hidden beneath the earth.
- Forge a lifelong Soul Bond with your very own Miniwyvern.

Capture full-sized dragons with Draconic Stones, then call them to your side with the Dragon Flute. Your companions are more than temporary summons—they grow with you, retain their unique identity, answer your commands, and can even be restored when they fall.

Your Soul-Bonded Miniwyvern is a companion unlike any other. Attune it to Fire, Ice, Lightning, Nature, Toxic, or Void, each with a distinct appearance, combat style, talent path, and magical bond. Whether it is scorching enemies, freezing threats, calling down lightning, protecting allies, spreading poison, or wielding the power of the Void, your Miniwyvern becomes a reflection of the path you choose together.

Every dragon brings its own personality to battle. Rain destruction from above, breathe fire across the battlefield, hurl crushing boulders, blanket enemies in poison, and fight beside a companion that becomes more powerful with every adventure.

And dragons were never meant to stay on the ground.

Mount your greatest companions, soar across the world, command devastating attacks from the saddle, and experience the freedom of dragon flight through powerful winged forms. Master the skies, strike from above, and turn every journey into an unforgettable adventure.

Gather rare Draconic Essences, forge stronger Draconic Stones, create the legendary Dragon Horn, restore fallen companions, and build an arsenal worthy of a true dragon master.

**Your dragon legend begins now.**
